Frequently asked questions

How can AI automation services help my business grow?

AI automation removes repetitive work from your GTM, sales, and support motions, qualifying leads, personalizing follow-ups, and running reporting around the clock, so your team can focus on strategy and relationships while the system scales output.

What types of processes can be automated with your AI services?

Lead ops (enrichment, scoring, routing), sales motions (outreach, follow-ups, CRM hygiene), marketing (content creation engines, distribution, nurture flows), and internal workflows like reporting and customer support triage.

Do I need to have AI expertise to work with MarketGrowth?

No. AI fluency is our job, not yours. We design, build, and manage the system, and translate the technical pieces into clear decisions you actually want to make.

What technologies and tools do you use for AI automation?

We work with n8n and Zapier for orchestration, Claude and OpenAI models for intelligence, and integrate with your existing stack: HubSpot, Salesforce, Segment, Clay, Apollo, and more.

Is my data safe when using AI automation?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access, use your own accounts and API keys wherever possible, and can deploy self-hosted options (like self-hosted n8n) so your data never leaves infrastructure you control.

How long does it take to implement an AI automation project?

Most Design+Build engagements ship an initial working workflow within 2–4 weeks, depending on scope and the number of integrations involved.

Can you integrate AI automation with our existing systems?

Yes, integration with your existing tools is the default, not an add-on. We build automations that plug into your current CRM, marketing, and data stack.
