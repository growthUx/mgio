FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is marketing operations?

Marketing operations is the system behind your marketing: the CRM, the data model, the buyer journeys, the campaigns, and the reports. We build that system in HubSpot and then run it for you.

Do you only work in HubSpot?

HubSpot is our primary platform. We build and manage Marketing Hub, Sales Hub, and Operations Hub. We also integrate HubSpot with Salesforce, Segment, Clay, Apollo, Stripe, and your product database.

What does a HubSpot CRM build-out include?

A build-out includes the object and property model, lifecycle stages, pipelines, lead scoring, list and segmentation logic, forms, email and landing page templates, workflows, dashboards, and data migration from your current tools.

What is buyer journey mapping?

We map each stage a buyer moves through, from first touch to closed deal. We then define the trigger, the content, and the owner for every stage. We build those stages into HubSpot as lifecycle stages, workflows, and campaigns.

Why does data modeling matter in a CRM?

Reports are only as good as the data model. We define the objects, properties, and relationships first, so attribution, forecasting, and segmentation stay accurate as your team grows.

Where does AI automation fit in?

AI automation sits on top of a clean CRM. Once the data model and journeys work, we add AI workflows for enrichment, lead routing, content production, and reporting. We use n8n, Zapier, Claude, and OpenAI models.

How long does a CRM build-out take?

Most CRM Build+Launch engagements go live in 2–4 weeks. The exact time depends on scope, the number of integrations, and the state of your current data.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We follow least-privilege access and we work in your own HubSpot portal and accounts. For AI workflows we can deploy self-hosted options, such as self-hosted n8n, so your data stays in infrastructure you control.
