The model

A studio, not an agency

An agency sells hours to other people's companies. A studio spends those hours on its own. We made that switch on purpose.

We spent a decade building go-to-market systems for other teams. The pattern repeated in every engagement. The hard part was never the strategy. The hard part was shipping the system and then running it.

So we kept the build capability and pointed it at our own companies. Today we run six. Each one serves a market where we already know the buyer, the workflow and the rules.

  • We start the company and we own it.
  • We build the product with a small team and an agent stack.
  • We operate it after launch. We do not hand it off.
  • We reuse every part across the next company.
