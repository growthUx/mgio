How it works

Three moves. No friction.

Sumi is deliberately small. Everything it does supports one act — you, writing.

01

Open a page

One keystroke and you're in a dark, silent room with your words. No toolbars, no sidebars, no red dots asking for attention.

02

Write in ink

Typography tuned like wet ink on paper. Sentences settle onto the page while Sumi stays completely out of the way.

03

Let it settle

Step away and Sumi quietly files drafts, versions, and threads. Come back to find your thinking already organized.

Products

One canvas, three instruments.

Every part of Sumi shares the same quiet surface — pick up whichever fits the moment.

Sumi Write

A distraction-free editor on an ink-dark canvas. Focus mode dims everything but the sentence you're shaping.

Daily pages

Sumi Journal

From draft to reader

Sumi Publish
